Email alerts

A preset is a saved set of job filters. After each job refresh, run with --notify and the app emails you one digest per preset: only postings that are new for that preset, best match first, each with its match %, a short reason and a link. A posting is emailed once per preset. Emails contain no resume text or personal facts, and go only from your Gmail to your own Gmail.
